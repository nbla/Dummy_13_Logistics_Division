Role

Compact expedition, trail and recreational crawler.

EV02 is based on the SCX30 platform and uses a pink pickup body configured with lightweight scale expedition cargo.

Unlike EV01, which developed through repeated body experimentation, EV02 reached a recognizable identity relatively quickly.

Its defining configuration combines:

- Pink pickup body
- Pink wheels
- INJORA M/T Maverix tires
- Roof-mounted canoe
- Scale cargo pallets

The result is a compact expedition vehicle with a considerably more cheerful appearance than its operational purpose requires.

No correction is considered necessary.
