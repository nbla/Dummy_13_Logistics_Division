Body Development

EV03's current Suzuki Jimny body was printed using a 0.4 mm nozzle.

A higher-detail replacement was later planned using a 0.2 mm nozzle.

The objective was straightforward:

Produce the same Jimny with finer surface detail for office display.

Repeated printer failures prevented successful completion of the replacement.

After multiple unsuccessful attempts, the 0.2 mm program was abandoned.

The original 0.4 mm Jimny therefore remains in active service.

This represents an unusual Logistics Division development outcome:

The existing component worked.

The proposed replacement did not.

The existing component was retained.

Development History

EV03 developed around a white Suzuki Jimny body printed using a 0.4 mm nozzle.

White wheels and INJORA Hamrok 1.0 tires established the vehicle's current running configuration.

Brass steering knuckles were installed as a functional chassis upgrade.

An antenna was added for scale detailing.

A higher-detail 0.2 mm replacement Jimny was subsequently attempted for the office configuration.

Repeated printer failures caused the replacement program to be abandoned.

The original 0.4 mm body remains installed.

EV03 therefore achieved its current configuration partly through successful development and partly through the printer refusing further negotiations.
