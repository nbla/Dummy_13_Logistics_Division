Vehicle Profile

Designation: EV01
Series: Expedition Vehicle
Platform: SCX30
Scale: 1:30
Wheelbase: 99 mm

Status: Active

Primary Body Family: Jeep Wagoneer

---

Role

Compact expedition, crawler and experimental development vehicle.

EV01 is the original SCX30-based Expedition Vehicle and has undergone several distinct body configurations during its service life.

The vehicle did not begin as a Wagoneer.

Its development progressed through multiple identities before the Wagoneer body eventually became sufficiently established that EV01 ceased functioning primarily as a general body experiment and became the Division's dedicated Wagoneer platform.

Broadly:

Green JLU
→ Nissan Patrol
→ Wagoneer
→ More Wagoneers

This development history is considered normal.

It probably should not be.

---

Original Configuration

Body:

Jeep JLU

Color:

Green

EV01 originally entered its development period using a green Jeep JLU body.

This represents the earliest established configuration of the vehicle before the more extensive printed-body experimentation that later came to define EV01.

The JLU configuration was eventually superseded.

Further details regarding this earliest configuration are currently limited.

---

Nissan Patrol Era

Following the original green JLU configuration, EV01 received a Nissan Patrol body.

The Patrol represents an important intermediate stage in EV01's development.

By this point, the SCX30 platform was increasingly being treated as a basis for experimenting with alternative scale bodies rather than simply retaining its original appearance.

The Patrol configuration was eventually superseded by the Wagoneer body family.

Detailed configuration records from the Patrol period are currently incomplete.

The body itself remains part of EV01's established development history.

---

Wagoneer Era

The introduction of the Jeep Wagoneer body substantially changed EV01's identity.

What began as another body experiment eventually became the vehicle's defining configuration.

Multiple Wagoneer bodies have since been produced, each allowing the same SCX30 platform to take on a substantially different appearance.

EV01 is now considered the dedicated Wagoneer vehicle within the Expedition Vehicle series.

Future general body experimentation is expected to occur primarily on other EV platforms.

Wagoneer experimentation remains permitted.

This distinction is apparently important.

The SCX30 platform provides EV01 with a very small physical footprint while retaining useful crawler capability.

EV01 became the first major development platform for this approach within the EV series.

---

Wheels and Tires

Current Tires:

Pro-Line Aztek

Current Status:

Operational

The Pro-Line Aztek tires are currently installed on EV01 and remain the vehicle's active tire configuration.

Their relatively large size gives EV01 a crawler-oriented stance and useful terrain capability.

Additional Wagoneer-oriented wheels have been produced, but suitable tires for these wheels have not yet been acquired.

Long-Term Wagoneer Standard:

INJORA M/T Maverix

Maverix tires are currently intended to become the standard tire choice for EV01's Wagoneer configurations once suitable sets are acquired.

Until then:

Aztek remains operational.

Maverix remains planned.

---

Clown

Body:

Jeep Wagoneer

Status:

Completed / Operational

Clown represents the most extreme development of EV01's Wagoneer configuration.

Established features include:

- Deliberately distressed and scorched appearance
- Chrome-style trim using approximately 1.5 mm steel rods
- Central turbo installation
- Two side-mounted fuel cans
- Roof rack
- Blue 2 mm front LEDs
- Red 2 mm rear LEDs

The LEDs are physically installed in the body.

They are not currently connected to operational lighting electronics.

The body was deliberately weathered using direct flame during construction.

This produced visible scorching and surface variation.

The procedure worked.

This fact has unfortunately prevented Logistics Division from using the outcome as evidence that the procedure was unreasonable.

---

Clown Operational History

Clown entered actual field use rather than remaining a display body.

A documented harbor outing demonstrated that the configuration remained fully operational despite its unconventional appearance and equipment.

Public response was positive.

No evidence suggests the central turbo contributed to vehicle performance.

This has not reduced its importance.

---

Display Wagoneer

Body:

Jeep Wagoneer

Print Configuration:

0.2 mm nozzle

Color:

Bone White

Status:

Completed / Display-Oriented Configuration

The Display Wagoneer represents a cleaner interpretation of EV01.

Established features include:

- Bone white body
- 0.7" bone-white beadlock wheels
- Roof rack
- Spare tire carried on roof rack
- Printed front mounting solution
- Clear 2 mm front LEDs
- Red 2 mm rear LEDs

The LEDs are physically installed in the body but are not currently connected to operational lighting electronics.

The roof-mounted spare tire forms an important part of the Display Wagoneer's expedition-oriented appearance.

The configuration is deliberately cleaner than Clown while retaining enough external equipment to remain recognizably Logistics Division.

---

Matte Black Wagoneer

Body:

Jeep Wagoneer

Color:

Matte Black

Status:

Completed

The Matte Black Wagoneer provides a substantially darker and cleaner interpretation of the EV01 Wagoneer configuration.

Established features include:

- Matte black body
- Clean black roof rack
- Orange 2 mm front LEDs
- Red 2 mm rear LEDs

The black roof rack is intentionally kept visually clean.

Rather than carrying significant cargo, its primary purpose is to add texture and variation to the vehicle's roofline and silhouette.

The LEDs are physically installed in the body but are not currently connected to operational lighting electronics.

The orange front lights provide a strong contrast against the otherwise dark configuration.

---

Reserve Wagoneer

Designation:

Clown #2

Status:

Future / Not Yet Produced

A future reserve Wagoneer body has been planned using a three-color filament-change scheme.

Planned color distribution:

Rear:
Approximately 35% Bone White

Middle:
Approximately 35–40% Military Green

Front:
Approximately 25–30% Bright Orange

Because the Wagoneer prints at an angle, filament changes are expected to create diagonal boundaries between the colors.

Bright orange filament is required before production.

The intention is to create another deliberately unconventional Wagoneer.

Lessons from Clown have therefore been documented.

Whether they were learned remains unclear.

Body Development History

Known EV01 body progression:

1. Green Jeep JLU
2. Nissan Patrol
3. Jeep Wagoneer
4. Clown
5. Display Wagoneer
6. Matte Black Wagoneer
7. Additional / reserve Wagoneer concepts

The JLU and Patrol configurations established EV01 as an experimental body platform.

The Wagoneer subsequently became sufficiently successful that experimentation produced a permanent identity.

EV01 is therefore no longer considered a general-purpose body test platform.

EV01 is the Wagoneer.

This has not reduced the number of bodies.
