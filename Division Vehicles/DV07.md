Vehicle Profile

Designation: DV07
Series: Division Vehicle
Platform: Redcat Everest Gen7 Sport
Scale: 1:10
Wheelbase: Adjustable, approximately 317–325 mm

Status: Active

---

Role

Trail, general off-road and rough-terrain vehicle.

DV07 is based on the Redcat Everest Gen7 Sport and represents Logistics Division's first operational 1:10 crawler platform.

The vehicle was acquired primarily as an inexpensive introduction to larger-scale trail vehicles while DV06 remains under construction.

Initial operation quickly established a more specific role.

DV07 is not intended primarily as a dedicated technical rock crawler.

Its operational emphasis is:

- Trail running
- Forest operations
- Rough terrain
- Beach and shoreline operations
- Moderate rock crawling
- General recreational abuse

DV07 is expected to become one of the less carefully preserved vehicles in the Division fleet.

This is intentional.

Body Color:

DV07 retains its original Redcat Everest Gen7 Sport body and appearance.

The body has a large, conventional SUV profile with black windows, substantial wheel openings and exposed crawler suspension beneath.

Unlike many Division vehicles, DV07 was not acquired with an immediate plan for extensive cosmetic modification.

This policy survived approximately until alternative bodies and roof cargo were discovered.

No changes have yet been made.

---

Transmission

DV07 uses a simple exposed drivetrain arrangement.

The pinion and spur gears are substantially open to the environment.

Initial concern was raised regarding operation in:

- Mud
- Sand
- Dirt
- Wet terrain

Investigation of other Gen7 vehicles showed that many owners operate the drivetrain in this configuration without additional protection.

No protective cover is currently planned.

Operational policy:

Use vehicle.

Inspect afterward.

Repair if necessary.

---

Operational Testing

DV07 has completed its first significant field operation.

Test environments included:

- Forest terrain
- Large rocks
- Shoreline
- Sand
- Wet surfaces
- Steep rock sections

The most immediate observation was the substantial difference between DV07 and the Division's smaller crawler platforms.

Terrain that previously required careful route selection could often simply be crossed.

Larger obstacles were required before technical driving became necessary.

DV07 successfully demonstrated the practical advantages of the larger scale.

---

Initial Assessment

DV07 was not considered perfect during its first operation.

Several aspects of the platform may eventually warrant modification.

However, the vehicle's approximately 2,030 SEK acquisition price substantially changes the assessment.

For that price, DV07 successfully provides:

- Complete 1:10 crawler platform
- Large-scale trail capability
- Significant terrain capability
- Immediate operational readiness
- Experience relevant to future DV06 development
- A vehicle that does not need to be treated carefully

The purchase is considered successful.

DV07 is an older, simple and inexpensive 1:10 crawler that entered Logistics Division service largely because the purchase price was difficult to ignore.

It is not the most advanced vehicle in its class.

Future upgrades will be based on actual operational requirements rather than internet recommendations.
