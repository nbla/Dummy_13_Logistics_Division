---

Role

Compact crawler and trail vehicle.

DV05 began life as an FMS FCX24 Max Smasher but has been deliberately configured away from its original monster-truck identity.

The underlying FCX24 drivetrain remains, including portal axles and a two-speed transmission.

Everything above it has considerably different ambitions.

The Suzuki Samurai body and smaller crawler-oriented wheel and tire configuration give DV05 a substantially more scale-oriented appearance than the original Max Smasher.

White beadlock wheels form an established part of the current configuration.

The original monster-truck appearance is no longer considered representative of DV05.

Platform

Drive Configuration:
4×4

Established platform features include:

- Portal axles
- Two-speed transmission
- Four-link suspension
- Compact crawler chassis

The FCX24 drivetrain provides a capable foundation for DV05 despite the platform's original Max Smasher configuration.

Low gear provides controlled low-speed operation and useful crawler torque.

High gear provides a considerably faster secondary operating range when technical crawling is not required.

Conversion History

FMS FCX24 Max Smasher
