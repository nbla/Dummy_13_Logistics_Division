Personality

Curious.

Inquisitive.

Unusually tactile.

Dust approaches unfamiliar objects and environments through direct interaction.

This is partly a matter of temperament.

It is also practical.

Dust is completely blind.

Rather than treating this as an obstacle to investigation, Dust frequently appears to compensate by touching, handling and physically examining unfamiliar objects.

The safety implications remain under review.

Appearance

Dust uses bone white armor over a black frame.

The combination gives the unit a stark, weathered appearance and is closely associated with the name Dust.

The name is also a deliberate reference to "Dust in the Wind."

The most distinctive feature is the empty lens position.

There is no damaged, obscured or inactive lens.

There simply is no lens installed.

---

Vision

Status:
Blind

Lens:
None installed

The physical mounting position for a lens remains present, but is empty.

Dust therefore has no conventional visual capability.

How Dust navigates and compensates for the absence of vision has not been technically established.

Observed behavior suggests that physical interaction with the environment forms at least part of the process.

A future lens installation remains possible.

Until such an installation occurs, blindness is considered Dust's normal operational state.

Notes

Dust's blindness is not currently treated as damage or a malfunction.

The unit was assembled without a lens and subsequently entered active service in that configuration.

Dust displays considerable curiosity regarding the surrounding environment.

This curiosity frequently involves physical contact.

Operational personnel are advised to consider whether unfamiliar objects are hot, sharp, fragile or otherwise unsuitable for investigation before Dust reaches them.

Dust does not currently appear concerned by this requirement.
