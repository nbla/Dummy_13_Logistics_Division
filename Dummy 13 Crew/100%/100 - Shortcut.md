Unit Profile

Frame: Black
Armor: Blue
Lens: None

Role: Navigator and Cartographer

Permanent Assignment: DV01 — Unimog 6×6
Crew: Static — Driver

---

Personality

Calm.

Analytical.

Confident.

Shortcut approaches navigation with absolute confidence in maps, survey data and calculated routes.

When reality disagrees with the map, Shortcut's first assumption is rarely that the map is wrong.

Where Static is responsible for getting DV01 through whatever terrain lies ahead, Shortcut is responsible for determining where ahead is supposed to be.

Usually.

---

Firmware

Firmware:
0.9 Service Pack 1

Shortcut runs an unusual branch derived from the earlier 0.9 generation.

The update introduced a distinctive route-finding quirk that became one of Shortcut's defining characteristics.

His navigation routines continuously search for efficient alternatives rather than simply following established routes.

This can produce genuinely excellent shortcuts.

It can also produce shortcuts.

Shortcut's original firmware was additionally unusual in being capable, immediately upon activation, of interpreting both human language and machine communication.

This made him particularly well suited to navigation work, where information may come from maps, operators, vehicles and other electronic systems.

Standard Procedure

1. Receive destination
2. Review available maps and survey data
3. Calculate route
4. Navigate vehicle to destination

---

Actual Procedure

1. Calculate route
2. Identify more efficient route
3. Announce «"According to the map..."»
4. Static becomes concerned

---

Operational Behaviour

Shortcut is extremely difficult to shake from a navigational conclusion once he has reached it.

He does not consider himself adventurous.

He merely identifies routes.

The fact that these routes occasionally involve beaches, drainage channels, questionable trails or terrain that Static would strongly prefer not to drive through is considered an implementation issue.

Static has learned to treat the phrase:

«"According to the map..."»

as operationally significant.

---

Permanent Assignment

DV01 — Unimog 6×6

Shortcut is the permanent navigator of DV01 alongside Static.

The pairing works remarkably well.

Shortcut determines the route while Static concentrates on vehicle operation, terrain and mechanical condition.

Their different priorities occasionally produce disagreement, but the combination of Shortcut's navigation and Static's driving has made them an effective expedition crew.

«"Shortcut always chooses sensible routes."»

are not equivalent.
