Primary Assignment

DV04 — WPL C24 Toyota

Relic serves as mechanic for DV04, the blue-and-white WPL C24 Toyota driven by Archive.

DV04 has undergone substantial changes during its service life.

Its original electronics have been replaced, with the vehicle now operating using an MT12 radio system.

The vehicle therefore mirrors its crew in an unusual way.

Archive and Relic originate from an early generation of Logistics Division hardware but remain operational.

DV04 is similarly an older platform that continues operating through maintenance, repair and replacement of systems rather than retirement.

Relic is responsible for making sure it stays that way.
